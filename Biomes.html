    Nexus CoreCost: NAUpkeep: NA

    Special: Reduces upkeep of all adjacent rooms by 1. The heart and soul of the dungeon and where you must take creatures and people in order to contract them to you. If this is destroyed, the quest ends

    Restrictions: Must always be located on the lowest floor

    Empty RoomCost: 5 ManaUpkeep: 1 Mana

    Special: Increases maximum mana by 4 points

    Boss RoomCost: 25 ManaUpkeep: 3 Mana

    Special: Closed Room. A single minion can be attached to this room, which then becomes a Boss Monster. See Boss rules for details

    Bait: 1 Uncommon Standard Chest

    Restrictions: Limit of 3 Horde and/or Boss Rooms per floor

    Horde RoomCost: 25 ManaUpkeep: 5 Mana

    Special: Closed Room. Up to 3X minions can be attached to this room, where X is the floor number this room is located. One of the attached minions must be designated as a Queen. See Horde Rules for more details

    Bait: 1 Uncommon Standard Chest

    Restrictions: Limit of 3 Horde and/or Boss Rooms per floor

    Mana CollectorCost: 10 ManaUpkeep: 2 Mana

    Special: Increases maximum mana by 7 points. Contains additional mana storage of 10 points that is strictly for mana from Invaders

    Restrictions: There can only be 1 opening into this room. Limit of 7 per floor

    Mana BeaconCost: 16 Mana and 6 EssenceUpkeep: Floor Number + 4 Mana
    A stream of pure energy-like mana erupting into the air circled by an array of ever-changing runes

    Special: Attracts Xd2 more Invaders to your floor each week, where X is the floor this room is placed on

    Bait: A vigorous flow of pure mana

    Restrictions: Limit of 1 per floor

    Healing CrystalCost: 7 ManaUpkeep: 1 Mana
    A large pulsating blue crystal

    Bait: A creature can spend 1 MP in this room to heal 1 minor wound or 1 MW to fully heal. The dungeon gains anything spent here as Mana or Essence, respectively

    Sacrificial RockCost: 10 Mana and 4 EssenceUpkeep: 2 Mana
    A bloodstained stack of round rocks

    Bait: Once an hour, an Invader can deal 1 moderate damage to themselves in this room in order to increase the rarity of all chests on the floor by one step. Damage taken to do this cannot be healed while the Invader is on the same floor

    Restrictions: Limit of 3 per floor

    Trap RoomCost: 6 ManaUpkeep: 2 Mana
    A nearly empty room with a slowly spinning gear in the ceiling

    Special: Increases the floor's number of trap slots by 1. A Mechanical trap placed will reset by itself after 30 minutes

    Restrictions: Limit 4 per floor

    Mechanism RoomCost: 8 Mana and 2 EssenceUpkeep: 3 Mana
    Constantly spinning gears and cogs line every wall of this room. In the center is a big red lever

    Special: Increases the floor's number of trap slots by 3. A Mechanical trap placed will reset by itself after 1 minute

    Bait: Pulling down the big red lever in this room deactivates all traps on this floor for the remainder of the day

    Restrictions: Limit 1 per floor

    Abominations Biome

    A massive hole-filled asteroid cluster floating in nearly empty space
    Cost: 22 Essence

    Local

    The upkeep of all Abomination minions is reduced by 2 and the upkeep of all Abomination rooms is reduced by 1

    The CBT of all Abominations is increased by 2

    Generates holes throughout the floor and walls that lead to empty space. If a creature falls through one of these holes, they will be deposited out the nearest Gravity Well. If the floor does not have a Gravity Well, they will be deposited at the entrance to the floor

    Provides unlimited food for Geovores

    All Standard Chests become Cosmic Chests

    Regional

    Changes the Light Level to Night. Floors with open-sky rooms or effects will have their sky set to a night sky full of stars

    The Initial Cost and Respawn Cost of all minions with Divine Basic Attacks is increased by 8 and cannot be reduced

    In the center of this room is a tall thin mountain-like structure with a meandering stair-like path up to the top. At said top rests an unadorned bronze altar. A hole in the ceiling looks onto the accretion disk of a supermassive black hole

    Special: All Abominations in this room gain +3 CBT. This bonus is increased to +6 CBT for 24 hours if a creature is killed and their blood spilled on the altar itself

    Bait: Crafting checks made at the altar gain a bonus equal to the CBT bonus the room is currently giving

    Restrictions: Abomination Biome only. Limit of 1 per floor

    Time Crystal GardenCost: 9 Mana and 1 EssenceUpkeep: 4 Mana
    A room filled with glowing clusters of cyan crystal

    Special: All Abominations in this room gain the Regeneration buff

    Bait: 1 Common Standard Chest

    Monument to MadnessCost: 9 Mana and 4 EssenceUpkeep: 3 Mana
    A ring of stone arches with various non-euclidean diagrams

    Special: All Abominations on this floor that are connected to Sigils gain +1 CBT and cost 1 less Upkeep

    Bait: 1 Common Cosmic Chest

    Restrictions: Limit of 1 per floor

    Oblivion MirrorCost: 9 Mana and 3 EssenceUpkeep: 3 Mana
    A slowly rotating ovular pitch black mirror hovers above a pedestal engraved with eyes

    Special: Evolutionary Room for Stellar Eyes and all creatures that evolve from them. The mirror can hold up to three attacks from creatures from the Stellar Eye tree. It can use one of these attacks as a combat action

    Bait: 1 Common Cosmic Chest under the mirror

    Sphere HiveCost: 8 Mana and 5 EssenceUpkeep: 2 Mana
    A polished asteroid cavern filled with large spheres of various hues

    Special: Evolutionary Room for Conglomerations of Spheres and most creatures that evolve from them. If a Conglomeration or Cocoon of Spheres is killed here, it immediately respawns with a health track of 1 severe wound at a cost of 2 mana to the dungeon. Such a respawn only happens once per creature per day

    Bait: 1 Common Cosmic Chest in the center. If a Conglomeration or Cocoon of Spheres is killed here, as long as that creature has not been killed in the room previously that day, this room's chest increases in rarity one step

    Sealed Opal DoorCost: 7 Mana and 3 EssenceUpkeep: 3 Mana
    A circular door carved from solid opal and covered in red runes is embedded in the floor of this room

    Special: Evolutionary Room for Creeping Cacophonies and most creatures that evolve from them

    Bait: 1 Uncommon Cosmic Chest behind the door. If the door is opened, creatures from the Creeping Cacophony tree on the floor gain the Aging trait. The door cannot be opened if there are no more such creatures on the floor

    Infinite StaircaseCost: 10 Mana and 4 EssenceUpkeep: 3 Mana
    This room is a mass of bizarre staircases that turn and twist and loop around in impossible ways

    Special: Evolutionary Room for Young Escher Beasts and most creatures that evolve from them. The linked Contractee and creatures from the Escher Beast tree can move into this room from anywhere as a combat action that costs 1 MP (Or 1 MW if outside the dungeon). Escher Beasts can bring along anyone caught in their Distortion Traps. The linked Contractee can bring along any one willing creature he or she may be touching

    Restrictions: Limit 1 per floor. Cannot be on a floor that doesn't have Escher Beasts

    Spiral TowerCost: 9 Mana and 3 EssenceUpkeep: 2 Mana
    A twisty black tower stretching up beyond the sky

    Special: Evolutionary Room for Living Spirals and all creatures that evolve from them. Mutation Source for the Spiral-Touched Mutation. Abomination bosses on this floor gain 2 extra Age stacks at the start of their final phase

    Restrictions: Limit 1 per floor. Cannot be on a floor that doesn't have Living Spirals

    Blasted HeathCost: 8 Mana and 2 EssenceUpkeep: 2 Mana
    A large circular area of heavy gray dust with a small meteorite embedded in the center

    Special: Evolutionary Room for Colours Out of Space and all creatures that evolve from them. Whenever a Colour or Colour-Touched creature in this room deals at least one moderate damage to an opponent, it gains one stack of Age. The damaged creature has an increased chance to gain the Colour-Touched Mutation from any Colours in the room

    Bait: 1 Uncommon Cosmic Chest buried underneath the meteorite

    Astral WindmillCost: 9 Mana and 2 Essence, Upkeep: 3Upkeep: Mana
    A sideways windmill of prismatic glass slowly spinning over the open void of space

    Special: Evolutionary Room for Haunting Cacophonies and all creatures that evolve from them. The linked Contractee and creatures that evolve from Haunting Cacophonies gain Immunity to any effect that would negatively alter or restrict their Movement

    Restrictions: Cannot be on a floor that doesn't have Cacophonies
