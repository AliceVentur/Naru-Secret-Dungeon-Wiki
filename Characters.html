Source

Age: 15

Favoured by: Earth's Heart

Affiliations: Celestial Custodian Clan (Clan Leader)

Known languages
  • Japanese (Fluent)
  • English (Decent)
  • Latin (Decent)
  • Aklo (Passable)
  • Egyptian (Poor)
Reputations
  • Gatherer of Heroes (Active)
  • Commander on the Battlefield (Active)
Special Abilities
  • Chaos Form
    • Naru can shift into Chaos Form at will. While in Chaos form, Naru gains the following:
      • 50% of LRN to CBT
      • The Hover, Compression, and Decompression traits
      • Basic attack changes to Eldritch (Bludgeoning)
      • Their melee attacks become short range
      • Cognitohazard: Anyone who sees them in chaos form must succeed at increasingly difficult SRT checks (Starts at DC 6 and increases by 6 each round spent in chaos form.) or be dealt 1 moderate horror. This is a Mental effect.
    • Naru can enter a lesser version of their Chaos Form by only partially transforming. While in partial chaos form, Naru gains the following:
      • 25% of your LRN to CBT
      • Cognitohazard: Anyone who sees them in partial chaos form must succeed at increasingly difficult SRT checks (Starts at DC 3 and increases by 3 each round spent in partial chaos form.) or be dealt 1 minor horror. This is a Mental effect.
    • While in full or partial Chaos Form, Naru can take a -3 CBT penalty for 1 full round in order to gain the Double Attack trait for 1 round
    • While in full Chaos Form, Naru can take a -6 CBT penalty for 1 full round in order to gain the Triple Attack trait for 1 round
    • While in full Chaos Form, Naru can spend 5 MP in order to gain the Double Action trait for 1 full round
  • Dungeon Nexus Master
    • Naru constantly knows everything that goes on in and around her dungeon (This trumps all forms of stealth and illusion)
    • +1 MP and +1 MW for every floor in the dungeon
    • Her Mana Well refills completely after a good night's rest
    • If she dies, she will be respawned at Naru's Nexus after one week
    • Granted Drain Resistance
    • Granted immunity to any effect that would target her material or cognitive souls
  • Soul Storage Technique
    • Naru can store anything in your soul that can fit through the size and shape of a star seed (i.e. material soul)
  • Overcasting Technique
    • Naru can use points from her Well in order to fuel spells that normally cost points from her Pool. The effects vary by spell and are unknown until Naru first Overcasts a spell
  • Infohazard Training
    • Naru gets a +6 LRN bonus when attempting to avoid Infohazards
    • If such a check comes up during Research, Naru rolls twice and takes the higher result
  • Cognitohazard Training
    • Naru gets a +6 SRT bonus when trying to resist Cognitohazards
    • If such a check comes up during Research, Naru rolls twice and takes the higher result
  • Warping Manifestation - The Upside-Down Lighthouse (Tier 2):
    • While using the Decompression trait in full Chaos Form, Naru may take up the same space as other creatures and objects (but not structures)
    • Other creatures are not hindered when moving through Naru
    • This ability is disabled if Naru is in the presence of a creature that has a higher tier Warping Manifestation ability
    • Primary Aspect: The Upside-Down Lighthouse (Rank 2)
      • Elements: Light, Emotion
      • Imposition: The area is bathed in golden-yellow light. Those touched by this light begin to emit glowing particles of either gold, silver, blue, or red. Rotating twin beams of light shine forth from the center of Naru. (Understandings 2/3)
        • Brilliant Defiance:
          • Light originating from Naru's central mass provides enough illumination to see clearly throughout the entire area in the scene, though shadows cast by objects that block this light are darker than usual
          • At the beginning of each round, all creatures in the scene gain a number of Courage stacks equal to Naru's Warping Manifestation Tier. This counts as the Courage Generation trait
          • Whenever Courage stacks would be removed from any creature due to succeeding at a SRT check, remove only half of the stacks instead of all (rounded up)
          • Whenever an ally would use Courage stacks to pay for the cost of a spell or innate ability, halve the number of Courage stacks required to pay the cost (rounded up)
          • This is an Eldritch (Light, Emotion) effect
        • Hope's Touch II:
          • When any ally is illuminated by light from your Imposition, they have a high chance to gain one of the following special Buffs at random. (Gold Lights being the most rare, Blue Lights being the most common.) Each ally can only benefit from this ability once per Scene. This is an Eldritch (Light, Emotion) effect
            • Golden Lights of the Champion: This creature's OCBT rolls have their results multiplied by 1.1 This buff can be expended as a Brief Action in order to spend all Courage stacks and gain 1 Age stack for every 2 spent (rounded down)
            • Silver Lights of the Defender: This creature's DCBT rolls have their results multiplied by 1.2 This buff can be expended as a Reaction to an enemy's attack against at least one ally within Short range. If used this way, this creature spends all Courage stacks and, for every 5 Courage spent, can have an ally within Short range ignore the attack entirely.
            • Blue Lights of the Healer: This creature's SRT rolls have their results multiplied by 1.2 This buff can be expended as a Combat Action in order to spend all Courage stacks and heal 1 minor damage from an ally in Short range for every 3 spent.
          • Once per round, as a Combat Action costing 1 MP or a Brief Action costing 3 MP, Naru can give an ally within her light her choice of one of the above special buffs and a number of Courage stacks equal to twice Naru's Warping Manifestation Tier
          • When any enemy is illuminated by light from your Imposition, they have a slight chance to gain the following debuff. Each enemy can only be affected by this ability once per scene. This is an Eldritch (Light, Emotion) effect
            • Red Lights of the Hunted: This creature gets -1 DCBT for every Courage stack it has. If this creature has had this debuff for at least one full round, it can dismiss it to deal 1 moderate damage to itself and exchange all Courage stacks for Wither stacks. The damage is an Emotion effect.
        • The Rumbling in the Distance
        • The Shaking Nearby
        • Rocks Fall, Things Die
      • Special: The Light and Emotion sub-types are added to the Focus Manifestation spell. The attack of the Focus Manifestation spell becomes an Area of Effect attack centered on the original target of the spell going out to Extended Melee range
  • Stance Change II:
    • Twice per round as a brief action, Naru can decrease either her OCBT or DCBT by up to 2 to increase the other CBT stat by the same amount.
  • White Dwarf Style I:
    • If in Chaos Form, while Stance Change has boosted Naru's DCBT 5 or more above its base, she gains the Aura Generation 3 trait.
    • If in Chaos Form, while Stance Change has boosted Naru's DCBT 12 or more above its base, Naru is considered to have the Counterattack trait against enemies that hit her with Melee attacks.
