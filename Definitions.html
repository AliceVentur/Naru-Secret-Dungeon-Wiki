Health
How much damage a creature can take before being defeated. This will be expressed as a number followed by the type of wound (i.e. 1 lethal wound, 3 moderate wounds, etc.). 2 minor wounds = 1 moderate wound, 2 moderate wounds = 1 severe wound, and 2 severe wounds = 1 lethal wound. If not stated, a creature or character's health is 1 lethal wound. A character that loses all Health is defeated and will die shortly after unless they get immediate medical attention or are otherwise healed by some effect. (Though note that some things will still kill creatures instantly.) A defeated character cannot normally re-enter combat even if they are fully healed
Sanity
How much horror an intelligent creature can take before going insane. Sanity has a similar track to health. 2 minor trauma = 1 moderate trauma, 2 moderate trauma = 1 severe trauma, and 2 severe trauma = 1 insane trauma. If not stated, a creature or character's sanity is 1 insane trauma. Unlike health, sanity is never explicitly stated. If you want to know how much sanity someone has left, you will need to infer it through context clues. Note that creatures who only have animal instincts do not have a sanity track. A creature that loses all its Sanity gains an Insanity Drawback
